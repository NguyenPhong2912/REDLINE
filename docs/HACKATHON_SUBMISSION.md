# Hackathon submission draft

## Project name

REDLINE

## Demo and repository

- Demo: https://csaclab.netlify.app
- Repository: https://github.com/anky06-ky/CSaCLAB

## One sentence

REDLINE is the programmable safety layer that lets AI agents move capital on Solana without crossing owner-defined limits.

## Problem

DeFi automation forces users to approve every transaction manually or grant a bot dangerously broad wallet access. Teams lack a standard way to bound an agent’s authority and prove what was approved before execution.

## Target user

Small crypto funds and DAO treasury operators that want automated monitoring and routine DeFi execution without giving an AI agent unrestricted custody.

## Competition track

Primary: Best Product & Business.

Best Technical Build becomes defensible after the included Anchor program is built, tested, deployed to Devnet, and integrated as the enforcement path.

## Product theme

AI × Web3, with DeFi & Digital Assets as the primary use case.

## Current progress

Working prototype: real Wallet Standard connection, Solana Devnet balance reads, AI-assisted risk assessment with deterministic fallback, and signed policy-digest publication through the Solana Memo program. Custom enforcement program source is included but not yet deployed.

## Biggest challenge

Turning policy proof into production enforcement across multiple DeFi protocols while keeping the agent useful, non-custodial, and safe under adversarial AI inputs.

## Mentor feedback requested

- MVP scope
- Solana / blockchain integration
- Technical architecture
- AI integration and guardrails
- Business model and design-partner acquisition
- Pitch and storytelling

## Suggested pitch opening

“AI agents need freedom to operate, but they should never have unlimited access to your money. REDLINE gives every agent an explicit boundary: what it can touch, how much it can spend, how often it can act, and when its authority expires—reviewed by AI plus deterministic rules and anchored on Solana before delegated execution. Programmatic enforcement is our next milestone.”
